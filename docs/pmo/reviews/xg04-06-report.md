# XG-04/06 report: the per-course "grades kept outside Canvas" answer, and the what-if for those courses (hand-off)

- **Status: hand-off (PR; the PMO merges).** A student can now tell Tally, per course, "This course's grades are kept outside Canvas: Automatic / Yes / No" (Course Detail's menu), and the answer reaches every consumer at once: the screens, the widget's glance (rewritten, widget reloaded), the digest at the next commit, a new session's coordinator, and the reminders' priority rule. For a course kept outside Canvas the what-if is back, as an estimate: the student types their real scores, reads the owner's approved disclaimer, and may set each category's weight; nothing is saved. The flagship's screens are unchanged.

## 4. Decisions and deviations

- **D1. The glance is rewritten at once**, not at the next commit (the brief's option). Otherwise the widget would contradict the Dashboard (which re-projects at once) until the next refresh. A new session starts from the stored answers without a rewrite.
- **D2. The estimate counts only what the student types** (no Canvas submission, no grading periods, every item worth points is a row, no baseline). The owner's decision is "type their real scores… see an estimated course grade"; mixing in Canvas scores would also show a grade the student said is not the real one.
- **D3. Totals follow Canvas's rule**, not a normalisation: under 100 the engine scales up (relative weights work); over 100 it does not (extra credit), so the sheet shows the total and a note. Per category, 0 to 100. Normalising every total to 100 is a one-line change in `OutsideCanvasEstimate.applying` if the owner prefers it.
- **D4. Blank categories in a points-based course** take their share of the points once another category is set (with nothing set the estimate is Canvas's points rule exactly). So setting one category usually makes the total differ from 100 until the others are set; the total and the over-100 note make that visible. (PMO's reading of the "universal default"; `defaultWeights` is the one place to change it.)
- **D5. A kept-outside course gets the estimate whatever its Canvas visibility** (BIO-H hides totals in Canvas): the scores are the student's own, so nothing the instructor withholds is revealed.
- **D6. "No estimate yet"** applies to every what-if with no grade after its answers land, so an early-term Canvas course (ART-1) no longer says "working it out" forever. Not the flagship (§5).
- **D7. `ReminderPipeline.swift` is not mine** (L10N-03a's lane), and it is where `ReminderSubjects` is built from the stored `UserState`. `ReminderSubjects` now applies the availability rule and accepts the answers, but the pipeline does not pass them yet, so reminders classify every course automatically: they agree with the Dashboard except for a "Yes" on a course that has Canvas scores (its items keep the near-boundary bonus, which only decides whether the last reminder is Time Sensitive; no grade is ever in a notification, R10). The change is one line, open item O1. A side effect of the rule itself: a course with hidden totals or letters only no longer gets a grade modifier in reminders, as on the Dashboard since XG-02.
- **D8. Shared or other streams' files, small and additive:** `TallyStrings/Resources/Localizable.xcstrings` (14 keys); `Account/AccountSessionFactory.swift` (one argument); `Home/HomeProjector.swift`, `Home/HomeModel.swift` (the plumbing the brief names); XG-03's `CourseDetail/*` and its hosted test `GradeNotInCanvasProjectionTests.swift` (two what-if expectations reversed by XG-06). Not touched: `L10n.swift`, `l10n-baseline.json`, every file on L10N-03a's list except `ReminderSubjects.swift` and the three `Settings/` plumbing files the brief gives me, `ci.yml` and every CI script.
- **D9. The hand-off is a PR, not a separate full run** (the PMO's updates during this package, which override the brief's rules 2 and 4): the report and journal are committed, `origin/main` @ `ac558b3` (PRs #20 and #21) is merged in (the journal kept both sides, main's entries first), the PR is opened against `main`, and its own run is the hand-off run. Not merged; the PMO merges. L10N-03a (PR #22) was still open at hand-off; a trial merge of `origin/l10n/sweep-a` @ `e09ba36` into this branch (`git merge-tree`) reported no conflict.
