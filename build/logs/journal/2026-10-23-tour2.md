## Step 6 — round 1's review: 4 of 9 legs needed a fix

- **testY (renewal-reminder banner): "missing-banner" on all 8 legs.** `SubscriptionEngine.apply`
  only plans renewal reminders `if verified, let environment, let facts`, and the `.launch`
  verification `start()` enqueues always runs before `accountAttached` (which never re-verifies
  itself), so a cold launch's one verified pass always has `environment == nil` — the banner could
  never have computed true. Fixed by backgrounding and reactivating the app after reaching the
  dashboard (the same `makeInactive`/`activate()` `testM_appLockAndPrivacyCover` already uses),
  which reaches `TallyApp.swift`'s `case .active: subscription.foreground()` — a second, now
  environment-attached verified pass.
- **testU (course goal), testX (canvas links picker): missing only on the 4 AX5 legs.**
  `scrollUntilHittable`'s plain default (10 swipes) and `scrollToTop`'s own default (20) are sized
  for ordinary rows; AX5's taller rows cover less list per swipe, so both ran out before reaching
  their target. Raised `scrollToTop` to 30 and the two `scrollUntilHittable` calls to 25 each
  (`testN`'s own `family.sample.viewAsParent`, deeper in the same Settings list, already needed
  30, so this is the same order of magnitude, not a guess).
- **testV (school-revoked Erase Saved Data confirmation): missing (silently — no file at all,
  success or failure) on all 4 AX5 legs.** My own bug: I'd copied testG's/testN's "or a popover"
  fallback for a `.confirmationDialog`'s odd size-class presentation onto
  `tallyDestructiveConfirmation`, which is only ever an `.alert` or a full-screen sheet — never a
  popover — so when the cancel button's 10 s wait ran out (plausibly just a heavier AX5 sheet
  render), the fallback found no popover either and recorded nothing. Replaced it with an explicit
  "missing-eraseConfirm" capture and raised the wait to 15 s, so this leg always records evidence.
