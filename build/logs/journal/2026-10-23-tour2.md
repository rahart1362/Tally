## Step 6 — round 1's review: 4 of 9 legs needed a fix

- **testY (renewal-reminder banner): "missing-banner" on all 8 legs.** `SubscriptionEngine.apply`
  only plans renewal reminders `if verified, let environment, let facts`, and the `.launch`
  verification `start()` enqueues always runs before `accountAttached` (which never re-verifies
  itself), so a cold launch's one verified pass always has `environment == nil` — the banner could
  never have computed true. Fixed by backgrounding and reactivating the app after reaching the
  dashboard (the same `makeInactive`/`activate()` `testM_appLockAndPrivacyCover` already uses),
  which reaches `TallyApp.swift`'s `case .active: subscription.foreground()` — a second, now
  environment-attached verified pass.
- **testU (course goal), testX (canvas links picker): missing only on the 4 AX5 legs.**
  `scrollUntilHittable`'s plain default (10 swipes) and `scrollToTop`'s own default (20) are sized
  for ordinary rows; AX5's taller rows cover less list per swipe, so both ran out before reaching
  their target. Raised `scrollToTop` to 30 and the two `scrollUntilHittable` calls to 25 each
  (`testN`'s own `family.sample.viewAsParent`, deeper in the same Settings list, already needed
  30, so this is the same order of magnitude, not a guess).
- **testV (school-revoked Erase Saved Data confirmation): missing (silently — no file at all,
  success or failure) on all 4 AX5 legs.** My own bug: I'd copied testG's/testN's "or a popover"
  fallback for a `.confirmationDialog`'s odd size-class presentation onto
  `tallyDestructiveConfirmation`, which is only ever an `.alert` or a full-screen sheet — never a
  popover — so when the cancel button's 10 s wait ran out (plausibly just a heavier AX5 sheet
  render), the fallback found no popover either and recorded nothing. Replaced it with an explicit
  "missing-eraseConfirm" capture and raised the wait to 15 s, so this leg always records evidence.

The other 5 legs (Q, R, S, T, W) were correct on every one of the 8 legs on round 1 — confirmed by

## Step 7 — round 2 (run 39994166321): all 8 legs green; re-diagnosed all 4 from their evidence

Every round-1 fix's own *theory* turned out to still be incomplete — but round 2's screenshots
each pinpointed the real cause precisely enough to fix with confidence, not a guess (rule 8):

- **testY:** still "missing-banner" everywhere, but the picture itself showed the banner rendered
  correctly, immediately visible, no scroll needed. `scrollUntilHittable` was simply the wrong
  primitive for "wait for an async verification to publish" — its only wait is the time its own
  swipe gestures take, and a fully on-screen element needs no swipe at all. Replaced with a plain
  `waitForExistence(timeout: 20)`.
- **testV:** still "missing-eraseConfirm" on every AX5 leg at a 15 s wait, but again the picture
  (taken either way — `snap`'s own settle runs regardless of branch) showed the confirmation
  dialog fully and correctly rendered. A genuinely slower AX5 sheet render, not a wrong
  presentation assumption. Raised the wait to 30 s.
- **testX:** "missing-menu" on 2 of 4 AX5 legs, and this time the picture showed the *unopened*
  Settings list — the tap genuinely did not open the menu. This is the identical timing flake
  `CanvasLinkPreferenceUITests` already fixes (PR runs #82/#83): wait for the menu's own option,
  re-tap once if it hasn't opened. Applied the same wait-and-retry here (without that file's
  `tap(_:expecting:)` helper's hard assertion, which this soft-fail tour never uses).
- **testU:** still "missing-stepperAfterWhatIf" on every AX5 leg, but now *overshooting* past the
  goal section to "Instructor", further down — round 1's fix (more swipes) made this worse, not
  better. `scrollUntilHittable` swipes back-to-back with no settle between them, unlike this file's
  own `captureScreenfuls`/`scrollToTop`; at AX5, consecutive unsettled swipes can carry enough
  momentum to skip a short section between two checks. Replaced with a hand-rolled settled-scroll
  loop using this class's own `waitUntilAtRest` (the same primitive `captureScreenfuls` already
  trusts at AX5 everywhere else in this file).
