## Invalidation (no imperative `.invalidate()` call — by design)

`.userActivity(_:element:_:)` retires a previously published activity once its element goes
`nil`. Two paths cover "invalidate on sign-out and in sample mode":
1. **Sample mode**: `webpageURL(...)` returns `nil` whenever `isSampleData`, unconditionally —
   verified sample fixtures (`canvas.northfield.example`) would otherwise pass `isSafe`, so this
   guard is load-bearing, not redundant with host validation.
2. **Sign-out**: `AppModel.signOut()`'s first step sets `route = .welcome`
   (`Shell/AppModel.swift:528-539`, "the Home's views go away and SwiftUI cancels their tasks"
   per its own doc comment) — `CourseDetailView` is only reachable under `.signedIn`, so it (and
   its `.userActivity` modifier) is torn down synchronously, before the async purge even starts.
