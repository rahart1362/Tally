## Step 3 — a11y #1 (run 38338079168, `ad7688f`): measured, clustered (2026-10-13)

Downloaded to `pmo-audit/.build-audit/mvp2b/a11y1-38338079168/` (129 files: JSON + PNG; the
`.window-audit` JSONs were lost to a naming bug, fixed below; scale is 3 px/pt, iPhone 17 Pro
1206 × 2622). `measure_run.py` / `annotate.py` / `contrast-table.md` there. 237 unwaived issues
(+6 in `calendar-light-ax5`, whose JSON is missing: the test failed to terminate the app at its end;
the log has them): 128 contrast, 69 dynamicType, 27 textClipped, 9 elementDetection, 2
sufficientElementDescription, 2 hitRegion.

What the evidence says, against the PMO's hypotheses:
- **Contrast: no colour token fails.** Of 131 contrast issues: 49 lie under the floating tab bar or
  its scroll-edge dimming (iOS 26's `AdditionalDimmingOverlay`, which starts 143 pt above the
  bottom; even fully visible text there, e.g. "Participation: September" at 15.3:1, is flagged),
  6 under the navigation bar / Calendar's pinned week strip, 8 partly below the screen edge, 3 are
  the disabled Reset button, 50 had no element at all (below), and 15 pass on their own pixels.
  Increase Contrast was really on: the restore link reads `#123C80` (accent's High Contrast value)
  there and `#1D4E9E` in Light. So no High Contrast token variant is needed; the glass button
  ("Find My School", 8.07:1) was never flagged.
- **Dynamic Type:** every finding at AX5 is a deliberate AX2 cap (SAMPLE DATA banner, Welcome's two
  actions, What-If's summary, toolbar buttons); the default-size findings on uncapped text
  (Settings' seven lower rows) are absent at AX5: the audit grows the text and re-measures, and
  rows pushed below the fold cannot be re-measured. `minimumScaleFactor` was never flagged.
- **textClipped** means "may be clipped at larger Dynamic Type sizes" (the detailed description):
  hand-off's address wraps mid-word ("ex- / ample", AX5 "can- / vas.north- / field.exam- / ple"),
  the paywall price is one word, What-If's summary/header in the medium-height sheet.
- **hitRegion:** `whatif.simulationLabel` is a combined element with no trait, read as tappable.
- **elementDetection** ("appears to display text that should be represented"): What-If's chips show
  "100%" but are labelled "Set Lab Report 4 to 100 percent".
- **In one `.all` audit, List screens returned contrast/clipping issues with no element** (the
  Dynamic Type pass had grown the text and the list rebuilt its rows): the passes are now split.
- `FamilyUITests`' prefix filter suppressed 11 (std) and 4 (AX5) issues: the banner's two capped
  texts and contrast on content under the tab bar. Nothing on the family's own elements.
