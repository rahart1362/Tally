  existing caption-row scale), `.accessibilityIdentifier("dashboard.momentumStreak")`. Renders
  nothing when hidden (the `RemindersTip` pattern at the same file's `RemindersTip`/
  `RemindersViews.swift:27-37`).

  `DueSoonSection` — "under... the Week-ahead section" per the brief.
