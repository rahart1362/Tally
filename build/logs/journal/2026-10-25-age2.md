## 2026-10-25 | Items 1-2: splitting the outcome (`AgeAssurance.swift`, `DeclaredAgeRangeAdapter.swift`, `AgeAssurancePorts.swift`, `AgeAssuranceModel.swift`)
- `AgeAssuranceOutcome` now has `.declined` (`Response.declinedSharing` only) and `.unavailable`
  (the OS floor, no presentation controller, a thrown error, `@unknown default`, and
  `UnavailableAgeAssurance`'s default). EB-01(g) unchanged: both still never block.
- `evaluate()`: `hasRequestedThisProcessLaunch = true` still happens before the call (at most one
  attempt per launch either way), but `recordStore.save(...)` moved to AFTER the result, gated on
  `result != .unavailable` — the record's date is still the request's start time (`now`, captured
  before the call), just not committed to disk for an attempt that never reached (or resolved for)
  the person. Chose "don't save" over "save then clear": `outcome` already carries the one signal
  needed, so no second "did it present" flag has to cross the adapter boundary.
- The significant-change acknowledgement guard gained `result != .unavailable`: a technical failure
  no longer consumes the one-shot trigger; `.declined` keeps today's behaviour (a real, resolved
  answer acknowledges the version).
