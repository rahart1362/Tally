     reminder: the planner's final "never schedule a past fire date" filter used `fireDate > now`,
     and event-fired reminders (grade posted / below goal) fire at `now` exactly via

     `now > now` is false, so they were silently dropped. Fixed the filter to `>= now` with a
     comment explaining why. Re-ran: **all 349 TallyDomainTests pass (4 pre-existing, unrelated
     `GradeParityTests` "known issues" only)**, all 195 TallyCanvasAPITests pass.
