Privacy Policy

Effective: __EFFECTIVE_DATE__ · Applies to the Tally iPhone app

The short version

  • There is no Tally account and no Tally server holding your data. Your Canvas information goes from your school's Canvas straight to your iPhone.
  • Tally never sees your school password. You sign in on your school's own Canvas page.
  • Your Canvas data on your iPhone is encrypted and replaced each time Tally refreshes. Sign Out & Erase deletes it.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we never sell data.

Information Tally uses and where it goes

Your Canvas information. This covers your courses, grades, assignments, due dates, calendar events, announcements and profile name. When you sign in, your iPhone requests it directly from your school's Canvas service (operated by your school and Instructure). Tally stores it only on your iPhone, encrypted with a key that never leaves the device, and replaces it on every successful refresh. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is excluded from iCloud and computer backups.

Sign-in. You sign in to Canvas in Apple's secure sign-in sheet, on your school's own page. Canvas gives Tally an access token that lets it read your information. The token is stored in your iPhone's Keychain, available only on this device. Signing out asks Canvas to revoke it.

Finding your school. When you search for your school, the app sends the search text to Instructure's public school directory. Tally also downloads a signed list of the schools where it is available from tally-app.dev. That is a static file; our web host may see your IP address as normal web traffic, and we don't receive or keep it.

Reminders and widgets. Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone; nothing is sent to a push server. By default, grade values are never shown on your Lock Screen or in notifications, and you can hide course names too.

Calendar. If you choose to subscribe to your Canvas calendar, your calendar app (for example Apple Calendar, Google or Outlook) fetches your personal Canvas calendar feed directly from Canvas. That provider's own privacy policy applies.

Purchases. Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles your payment details. Tally only checks, on your iPhone, whether you have an active subscription. Apple's privacy policy applies to purchases.

Parents and linked accounts

A student can invite a parent or guardian using Canvas's own pairing codes. The parent signs in with their own Canvas observer account; a student's password or token is never shared. A parent sees what Canvas already allows observers to see. That information is fetched directly from Canvas to the parent's iPhone and stored encrypted there. Students can see who is linked to their Canvas account in the app. Only the parent or the school can remove a link, because Canvas works that way.

A student can also share a weekly or monthly summary with anyone they choose. Nothing is sent until the student taps Share or Mail and picks who receives it.

Children

Tally is used by students who may be under 18. Tally has no accounts of its own and does not collect personal information on any server.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Sign Out & Erase (Settings) revokes the Canvas token and deletes all Tally data, keys and scheduled reminders from your iPhone.
  • Deleting the app also deletes its data.
  • Subscriptions are managed and cancelled in your Apple Account settings.

Security

Tally uses Apple's built-in encryption and Data Protection. Tokens are kept in the Keychain on this device only. You can require Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode to open Tally.
