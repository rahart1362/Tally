For students whose school uses Canvas

Know where you stand. Know what's next.

What you get

Your whole term, at a glance.

Tally reads your grades, assignments and due dates from your school's Canvas account and lays them out on your iPhone, so one look answers two questions: how am I doing, and what's next? The Dashboard shows your overall standing and its trend. Next up puts the most important work first, and Needs attention flags anything missing or overdue.

Courses

Every class, with its own trend.

Each course shows its current grade, a trend, and what's due next.

Course detail breaks the grade down by category and weight, following Canvas's own grading rules.

What-If

See what you need on what's next.

Try scores on upcoming work and watch your projected grade change.

What-If never changes anything in Canvas. It's a calculator on your iPhone, clearly marked as a simulation.

To-Do and Calendar

Everything due, in one list.

Work from every course in one place, with missing and overdue items first and a clear note when late work is still accepted.

Add any assignment to your iPhone's Calendar with one tap. Or subscribe to your Canvas calendar feed so it stays current on its own.

Insights

See the term, not just today.

Your performance trend over a month, three months or the whole term, plus what your grades are made of.

A week-ahead view shows what's coming.

Beyond the app

Right where you already look.

Reminders

A notification a day before and an hour before each deadline, by default. Notifications never show grades.

Widgets

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets for what's next, due soon and the week ahead, with a one-tap "mark done." Grades appear only if you turn them on.

Shortcuts and Siri

Ask what's due, or add a Tally shortcut to your day.

Dark mode and large text

Designed for dark mode, and readable up to the iPhone's largest text sizes, with VoiceOver support.

Privacy

Private by design.

Your Canvas information goes from your school's Canvas straight to your iPhone. It's encrypted there and replaced each time Tally refreshes, and it's never sent to us or anyone else.

0Tally accounts. You sign in with your school's Canvas.
0Tally servers holding your data.
1encrypted copy, on your iPhone.
  • You sign in on your school's own Canvas page, and Tally never sees your password.
  • Optional App Lock with Face ID.
  • Settings → Sign Out & Erase removes everything Tally keeps on your iPhone.
  • No ads and no data sales, ever.
