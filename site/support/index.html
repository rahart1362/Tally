Common questions

"Tally isn't available at my school yet." Your school's Canvas administrator needs to enable Tally. In the app, tap Ask My School; it creates a message you can send to your school.

"It says it's showing saved data." Tally shows your last saved information whenever Canvas is slow or you're offline, then updates automatically. Pull down or tap the refresh button to try again.

"Tally asked me to sign in again." Your school's Canvas sessions expire. Tap Sign In. Usually one tap on Continue is enough.

Subscriptions and refunds are handled by Apple: see Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions, or request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Deleting your data: Settings › Sign Out & Erase, or delete the app.
